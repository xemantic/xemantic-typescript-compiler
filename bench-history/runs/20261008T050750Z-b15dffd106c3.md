The AOT image is expected to TRAIL the warm JVM (measured 8.4 s vs 6.5 s on the dev box) — its value is that it needs no warm-up at all, so it is the one-shot CLI answer while the warm JVM is the daemon answer (docs/perf/aot-native-image.md). NOTE: CI publishes the NON-PGO base image here; the shipped artifact (native.yml, Oracle + PGO) is ~14% faster check-only and ~20% faster on emit.
